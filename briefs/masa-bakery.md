# Client brief — Masa Bakery

**Client:** Masa Bakery (family-owned sourdough bakery)
**Location:** 1214 East 6th Street, Austin, TX — open Tue–Sun, 7am–3pm
**Contact:** hello@masabakery.example · (512) 555-0142

## About the business
Masa was founded in 2019 by siblings Lucía and Diego Ortega, who grew up baking pan dulce
with their grandmother in Monterrey. Today they bake naturally leavened sourdough, conchas
made with a 48-hour fermented dough, and seasonal pastries using flour milled in Texas.

## Goals for the new website
1. Get more people to pre-order bread for weekend pickup (currently handled by phone).
2. Promote their Saturday sourdough workshops ($65 per person, 8 seats, monthly).
3. Win wholesale clients: local cafés and restaurants.

## Audience
Austin locals aged 25–45 who care about real ingredients; café and restaurant owners.

## Brand & tone
Warm, artisanal, a little playful. Proud of the Mexican roots without being cliché.
Avoid corporate words like "solutions" or "leverage".

## Must have
- Menu highlights (sourdough loaves, conchas, seasonal pastries)
- Workshop info and how to sign up
- Wholesale inquiry
- Location, hours and contact
