# Ledgerly — marketing site brief

- Product: Ledgerly, bookkeeping automation for US freelancers and solo agencies
- What it does: connects to bank accounts, auto-categorizes expenses, estimates quarterly taxes, exports to TurboTax
- Stage: public beta, launching v1 in Q1
- Pricing: Free plan (1 bank account), Pro $12/mo (unlimited accounts, tax estimates), 14-day Pro trial
- Primary goal: beta sign-ups (free plan)
- Secondary goal: waitlist for the upcoming "Ledgerly for Teams"
- Audience: freelance designers, developers, writers; 1–5 person agencies; hate spreadsheets
- Differentiator: built specifically for 1099 income, plain-English tax estimates, no accountant jargon
- Security: bank connections via Plaid, read-only access, SOC 2 Type I in progress (NOT certified yet — do not claim certification)
- Tone: friendly, clear, confident, light humor ok
- Pages they asked for: home, features, pricing, security, and an FAQ
- No blog for now
